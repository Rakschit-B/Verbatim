VERBATIM 3.3 by Infinite Story — Mac (Apple Silicon: M1/M2/M3/M4)

INSTALL
1. Drag VERBATIM onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click VERBATIM.

IF macOS SAYS IT "CANNOT BE OPENED" OR "CANNOT VERIFY THE DEVELOPER"
This build is not yet notarised by Apple, so macOS asks once:
  macOS 15 Sequoia / macOS 26: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
  click "Open Anyway" next to VERBATIM, then confirm with your password.
  macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click VERBATIM > Open > Open.
You only do this the first time.

FIRST USE
- English works offline immediately: NVIDIA Parakeet is built into the app.
- Hindi and other languages use Whisper turbo, downloaded once (1.6 GB). To get it ready in
  advance, open Settings (left menu) > Speech models > "Download recommended models…".
- When newer speech models are released, VERBATIM tells you and asks before downloading.
- No Python, Homebrew or Terminal needed — VERBATIM carries everything inside it.

UPDATES
- VERBATIM shows a banner when a new version is available.
- Optional: double-click "Schedule Weekly Updates.command" for a check every Monday.

Your transcripts and the MASTER_TRANSCRIPTION_LOG.xlsx live in Documents/Infinite Story Transcripts
(or the folder you choose) and are never touched by updates.
